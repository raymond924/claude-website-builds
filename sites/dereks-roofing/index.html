FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Still have a question? Call or WhatsApp 071 250 0125 and you'll speak to Derek.

Which areas do you service?

We're based in Welgemoed and mainly serve Cape Town and the Northern Suburbs, including Bellville, Durbanville and Tyger Valley. A call-out fee applies outside the Northern Suburbs, depending on the area, so contact us to confirm your location.

How quickly can you give me a quote?

We aim to reply within one business day and can usually arrange an on-site inspection within a week.

Do you offer emergency leak repairs?

Yes. Call or WhatsApp 071 250 0125 to report an urgent leak and we'll respond as quickly as possible.

Do you provide a guarantee on your work?

All work is done to industry standard. If you notice a problem with completed work, let us know promptly so we can assess it and put it right.

What roofing materials do you work with?

Tiled, IBR sheeting and shingle roofs, plus flat-roof waterproofing, timber decking and pergola structures.

Do I need to be home for the quote?

It helps to have someone there to give access and answer questions, but it isn't always necessary. Let us know what suits you.
