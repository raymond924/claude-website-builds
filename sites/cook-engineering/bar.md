# The bar: T1 Energy homepage

Gold standard: [Refero style e79b761d](https://styles.refero.design/style/e79b761d-f476-4c5d-8943-e31a58664e4d),
measured on the live page at [t1energy.com](https://t1energy.com) (1440px, scrolled capture, 2026-10-07).

Section order on the live page: full-bleed video hero → Mission → two-up location video tiles →
Manufacturing statement + product panel with toggle → Supply chain → Technology (render + accordion on a
vellum band) → Company + big statistics → News grid → onyx footer.

## Mechanisms (each one checkable by looking at a screenshot)

1. **Split-column grammar.** Every content section puts a tiny label (12px monospace, uppercase, a 4px
   solid square before it) in the far-left column, and starts its text at the horizontal midpoint of the
   page. The left half stays empty apart from that label.

2. **Few sizes, one light weight.** Text uses only 12px mono labels, 14px UI text, 24–32px statements and
   one display size (52px hero, 96px statistics). Statements and display are weight 300. Nothing on the
   page is bold.

3. **Media goes edge to edge with big rounded corners.** Photos and videos are either the full-bleed hero,
   whose bottom corners round to about 80px, or tiles with an 80px radius that run to within about 8px of
   the viewport edge, two side by side with an 8px gap. No media has a frame, border, caption box or shadow.

4. **No colour in the interface.** UI surfaces are only white, warm vellum (#f0efe9), charcoal and near-black
   onyx. Vellum appears once, as a full-width rounded band behind the technology section. Every saturated
   colour on screen comes from photography or video.

5. **Controls sit on the media as translucent dark pills.** The fixed nav is a dark pill at about 50%
   opacity, top right. Video tiles have a centred location pill with a circular arrow button. The product
   panel has a two-option segmented toggle (Cell / Module). Text buttons are outlined pills.

6. **Air.** Sections are padded 60–120px top and bottom. On any screen outside the hero, at least about 40%
   of the frame is empty canvas, not counting the media.

7. **Index footer.** The footer is a full-width near-black block with link columns numbered like a document
   (1.1, 1.2 … 2.1, 2.2 …) in 12px uppercase mono, plus an oversized white logo mark in the bottom-left.
