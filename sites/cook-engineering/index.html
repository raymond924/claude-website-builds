Technology

Five stages, one continuous film, from single-strength juice to frozen concentrate.

  • Single-strength juice in

    Juice is preheated with vapour recovered from the effects, so heat that leaves one stage warms the feed for the next.

  • Short high-temperature hold

    A brief hold inactivates pectin methylesterase and spoilage organisms, so the finished concentrate keeps a stable cloud.

  • 4–7 effects · optional TVR

    Juice runs as a thin film down the tube walls of each effect. Residence time stays short and the temperature drops effect by effect, which protects flavour and colour.

  • Essence column

    Volatiles stripped from the first-effect vapour are condensed and rectified into an essence that can be added back or sold separately, while the last effects finish the juice.

  • Concentrate out

    Concentrate is flash-cooled under vacuum and pumped to blending or drumming, ready for frozen storage.
