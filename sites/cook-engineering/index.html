Evaporators

Juice concentrators built for citrus

Gentle, short-residence evaporation that takes single-strength juice to frozen concentrate while protecting colour, flavour and aroma.

01Feed & preheat

Single-strength juice is preheated with recovered vapour, so the energy that leaves one effect warms the feed for the next.

02Pasteurisation

A short high-temperature hold inactivates pectin methylesterase and spoilage organisms before concentration, giving a stable cloud in the finished concentrate.

03Falling-film effects

Juice runs as a thin film down the tube walls of each effect. Residence time stays short and temperatures drop effect by effect, which protects flavour and colour.

Thermal vapour recompression cuts live-steam demand further on larger units.

04Aroma recovery

Volatile aromas stripped in the first effect are condensed and rectified into an essence that can be added back to reconstituted juice or sold separately.

05Flash cooling & discharge

Concentrate is flash-cooled under vacuum and pumped to blending or drumming, ready for frozen storage.
