Technology

Five stages, one continuous film, from single-strength juice to frozen concentrate.

2–25 t/h · 4–7 effects · optional TVR · 316L · CIP

  • Single-strength juice in

    Recovered vapour preheats the incoming juice.

  • Short high-temperature hold

    Inactivates enzymes so the concentrate keeps a stable cloud.

  • 4–7 effects · optional TVR

    Juice runs as a thin film down the tube walls; short residence protects flavour and colour.

  • Essence column

    Volatiles are condensed into an essence to add back or sell.

  • Concentrate out

    Cooled under vacuum and pumped to blending or drumming.
