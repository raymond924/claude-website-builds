How we work, stage by stage

We follow the RIBA Plan of Work, so architects, contractors and clients always know which decisions are fixed and which are still open.

  1. 0

    Strategic definition

    Constraints, risks and the brief behind the brief.

  2. 1

    Preparation

    Desk study, surveys and ground investigation scope.

  3. 2

    Concept

    Two or three structural schemes, costed and carbon-rated.

  4. 3

    Spatial coordination

    One federated model. Clashes resolved before stage 4.

  5. 4

    Technical design

    Full calcs, drawings and specifications for tender.

  6. 5

    Manufacturing & construction

    Site inspections, RFIs and temporary works review.

  7. 6

    Handover

    As-built records and the Health & Safety file.

  8. 7

    Use

    Post-occupancy monitoring and future alterations.

Stages 0–2 carry 80% of the cost and carbon outcome. Involve us early.
