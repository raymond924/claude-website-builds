A farm stay
made for slowing down

Lindani is a small farm just outside Paarl, at the foot of the mountains. A tree-lined road brings you in, and the city stays behind at the gate.

There are four private self-catering studios, each with its own patio. Days are slow here: a swim in the pool under the old trees, a walk down to the dam, coffee in the garden.

Evenings are for the braai, and for watching the cliffs turn gold. Nothing to rush for, and nobody to rush you.

When you do want to go out, Fairview, Nederburg and Franschhoek are close by. Plan less, explore more.
