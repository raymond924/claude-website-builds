Questions

Straight answers.

Something else on your mind? Call us; a roofer answers, not a call centre.

Do I need a new roof, or will a repair do?

Most roofs we inspect need a repair, not a replacement. If the trusses and underlay are sound we'll replace broken tiles, re-point the ridges and fix the flashings. We only quote a re-roof when the photos show it's needed.

Will my insurance pay for hail damage?

Sudden storm and hail damage is covered by most South African home-owner policies; wear and tear isn't. Our report separates the two clearly, which is what assessors look for.

Can you work while I'm living in the house?

Yes. We open only the section we can close the same day and cover it with sheeting overnight, so the house stays dry and liveable throughout.

Do you need council approval for a re-roof?

Like-for-like replacements usually don't. Changing the material or the roof structure may need approved plans; we'll tell you at the inspection and can manage the submission.

Which areas do you cover?

Johannesburg, Pretoria, Centurion, Midrand and the East and West Rand. Further afield for larger commercial and estate projects.
