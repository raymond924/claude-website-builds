# Bar: graftonsafaris.com homepage (captured 2026-10-10, 1440 and 390 wide)

Reference frames: `bar/desk-00.png` … `bar/desk-16.png`, `bar/mob-00.png` … `bar/mob-12.png`.
What makes it work, as checkable mechanisms:

1. **Inset rounded panels on a warm off-white page.** The page is #FAF9F8. Every big block (hero, the dark "Unrivalled" band, the closing form, the footer) is a rounded panel (about 12–16px radius) inset about 12px from the screen edges. Nothing heavy runs full-bleed.

2. **One light display serif, huge against tiny body text.** Headings are a thin, high-contrast serif at 72–96px on desktop, at least 5× the body text (13–14px sans, line-height about 2). Each major heading has one italic phrase as its only accent ("The Grafton *Experience*", "*you'll never forget*"). Three text sizes do almost all the work: display, body, tiny uppercase label.

3. **One saturated colour, booking actions only.** The UI is greys, sage (#DCDED8) and charcoal (#222). The one strong colour (orange) appears only on booking/action buttons: the nav "Book now" and the small "Play video" buttons. At most two per screen.

4. **The booking form is the first thing after the hero, and it closes the page.** A white card overlaps the hero's bottom edge, with a large serif heading ("Start your adventure here") and one row of fields. Fields are borderless grey fills with tiny bold uppercase labels. The same form repeats at the bottom on a dark photo panel ("An experience *you'll never forget*").

5. **Offers as a giant faint serif list.** Package names are about 40px light-grey serif with tiny "( REGION )" tags in brackets. The active item is dark; the rest stay faint. It ends with a "label + circled arrow" link.

6. **Paired cards that stack.** Each destination is a sage text card (title and body at the bottom-left) beside a photo card of equal height, both 12px radius, with a 12–16px gap. As you scroll, the next pair slides over and the previous one tilts slightly.

7. **Quiet chrome and links.** A floating white nav bar, inset from the edges, with small centred links and one filled CTA on the right. Secondary links are always "Label ⟶" with a thin 40px circled arrow, never underlined text. The footer is a charcoal rounded panel with phone and email in the display serif (about 24px) and a large circled up-arrow.
